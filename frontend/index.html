Frequently asked questions

How is PulseCheck different from UptimeRobot? ↓
UptimeRobot offers basic monitoring but no built-in status pages, no incident management, and their UI hasn't been updated in years. PulseCheck combines monitoring + status pages + incident tracking in one clean tool, and we're actively maintained. We're also ~50% cheaper than UptimeRobot Pro for teams.
Do I need to host anything myself? ↓
No. PulseCheck is fully hosted SaaS. Sign up, add your monitors, done. If you prefer self-hosting, PulseCheck is open-source — you can run the entire stack with Docker Compose in under 5 minutes.
How quickly will I be alerted when my service goes down? ↓
On the Pro plan, checks run every 60 seconds. We require 3 consecutive failures before triggering an alert to eliminate false positives — so worst case is 3 minutes. On Agency plan with 30-second intervals, that's under 90 seconds.
Can I migrate from Pingdom or StatusPage? ↓
Yes. We offer a CSV import for bulk monitor creation and our team can help you migrate your status page subscribers. Most migrations take under 30 minutes.
Is there a free trial for paid plans? ↓
Yes. All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You'll be prompted to add payment details only at the end of the trial period.
